The album

A confession written in silence.

SALIERI’S HANDS is a KAMDRIDI classical / dark baroque off-series album.

It uses the Salieri myth as a dramatic symbolic frame for original KAMDRIDI compositions: faith, envy, devotion, confession, regret and immortality.

This is not a historical biography. It is a cinematic musical interpretation: a dark baroque world where the figure of Salieri becomes a symbol of artistic burden, guilt, jealousy and prayer.
